Privacy Policy

NutriPilot — Macro Tracker for Body Recomposition
Last updated: June 2026

Your health data belongs to you. NutriPilot does not sell, share, or monetize your personal information. Data is stored securely and used only to make the app work for you.

1 · Who We Are

NutriPilot is an iOS application developed and operated by Cyril Daime ("we", "us", or "our"), an independent developer. You can contact us at cyril.daime@gmail.com.

This Privacy Policy explains what data NutriPilot collects, how it is used, and what rights you have over your data.

2 · Data We Collect

Account data

When you create an account, we collect your email address and a hashed password. No name, phone number, or social login is required.

Body & health data

If you use the progress tracking features, we store the data you voluntarily enter:

  • Body weight (kg or lbs)
  • Body fat percentage
  • Muscle mass
  • Your TDEE-based targets (calories, protein, carbs, fat)

This data is linked to your account and stored on our servers. It is never shared with third parties for advertising or profiling purposes.

Food & nutrition logs

We store the meal and nutrition entries you log, including:

  • Foods, dishes and quantities consumed per meal slot
  • Daily calorie and macro totals
  • Day type (Deficit, Refeed, Diet Break)
  • Hydration logs

Custom foods and recipes

Foods, dishes and recipes you create are stored in your personal library and are private to your account.

Device & usage data

We collect minimal technical data to keep the app functioning correctly:

  • App version and operating system version
  • Crash reports and error logs (anonymised)
  • Subscription status (Pro or Free)

We do not track advertising identifiers, browsing behavior, or usage analytics beyond what is strictly necessary for the app to function.

3 · Third-Party Services

Supabase (database & authentication)

NutriPilot uses Supabase to securely store your account and health data. Supabase is hosted on AWS infrastructure in the EU region. Supabase acts as a data processor on our behalf and does not access or use your data independently.

Supabase Privacy Policy: supabase.com/privacy

RevenueCat (in-app purchases & subscriptions)

Subscription management (NutriPilot Pro) is handled by RevenueCat. RevenueCat processes your App Store transaction data to verify and manage your subscription. Payment details (credit card, Apple ID) are handled entirely by Apple and are never transmitted to us or RevenueCat.

RevenueCat Privacy Policy: revenuecat.com/privacy

Open Food Facts (barcode scanner)

When you scan a barcode, the barcode number is sent to Open Food Facts, an open-source public food database, to retrieve nutritional information. No account data or personal identifiers are included in these requests.

Open Food Facts Privacy Policy: openfoodfacts.org/privacy

Apple (App Store, StoreKit)

All payments are processed by Apple. Apple's privacy practices are governed by the Apple Privacy Policy.

4 · How We Use Your Data

We use the data we collect exclusively to:

  • Provide the core functionality of the app (logging meals, tracking body metrics, generating macro targets and meal suggestions)
  • Authenticate you securely and sync your data across devices
  • Manage your subscription status
  • Diagnose and fix technical issues

We do not:

  • Sell your data to any third party
  • Use your health or nutrition data for advertising
  • Share your data with data brokers or analytics platforms
  • Profile you for purposes beyond delivering the app's core features

5 · Data Retention

Your data is retained for as long as your account exists. If you delete your account, all associated data (logs, custom foods, body metrics, account information) is permanently deleted from our servers within 30 days.

6 · Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Access — request a copy of the data we hold about you
  • Rectification — correct inaccurate data
  • Erasure — request deletion of your data ("right to be forgotten")
  • Portability — receive your data in a structured, machine-readable format
  • Objection — object to the processing of your data

How to delete your account

You can delete your account and all associated data directly from the app:

Profile → Settings → Delete Account

Alternatively, send an email to cyril.daime@gmail.com with the subject "Account Deletion Request" and we will process your request within 30 days.

7 · Data Security

All data transmitted between the app and our servers is encrypted using HTTPS/TLS. Passwords are hashed and never stored in plain text. Access to the database is restricted and protected by Supabase's Row Level Security (RLS) policies, ensuring each user can only access their own data.

8 · Children's Privacy

NutriPilot is designed for adults aged 17 and older and is not intended for use by children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has provided us with their data, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

9 · Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Significant changes will be communicated via an in-app notice or email. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page reflects the most recent revision.
